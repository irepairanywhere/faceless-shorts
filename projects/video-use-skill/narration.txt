You can now edit videos by just talking to Claude Code.

Save this before you pay another editor for a simple cut.

The team behind Browser Use just open-sourced a skill called video-use.

You drop your footage in a folder and describe the edit in plain English.

It transcribes every word with timestamps, finds the silences, and cuts on word boundaries, so nothing gets chopped mid-sentence.

Retakes, filler words, dead air: gone. Then it grades the color, burns in captions, and drops in B-roll where it makes sense.

I ran my own ad footage through it. It found the clean take, removed the stutters, and rendered the finished cut in seconds.

And it remembers every decision in a project file, so the next edit starts where the last one ended.

Free. Open source. Runs on your own Mac.
